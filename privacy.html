Slime Merge — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

The short version

Slime Merge does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no trackers.

What stays on your device

Your best score, the slimes you have found, your sound setting, the name you type for challenges, and scores from challenge links you open are saved only on your own device. Deleting the app (or clearing the site's data) removes them.

Challenge links

When you choose to send a challenge, the link contains the game seed, your score and the name you typed. It goes only to the people you share it with, through the share method you pick. We do not receive it.

Children

The game collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
